How it works

Four easy steps to swap toys with your friends.

📝

1. Register

Sign up with your name and a 4-digit PIN. You start with 🪙 500.

📸

2. List toys

Add photos of toys you're bored with and set a coin price.

🙋

3. Ask to buy

See a toy you like? Ask to buy it. The owner says yes or no.

🤝

4. Meet up

When they say yes, coins move. Swap the real toys when you meet!
